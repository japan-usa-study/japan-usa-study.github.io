Financial Support

Scholarships and funding opportunities for your studies

Pursuing higher education in the United States can be expensive, but there are numerous scholarships, grants, and funding opportunities available to help international students achieve their academic dreams. Here's an overview of financial support options:

Scholarships for Japanese Students

Scholarships are a great way to reduce the financial burden of studying abroad.

General Scholarships

GENERAL

Fulbright Foreign Student Program

A prestigious scholarship for graduate students, artists, and professionals from over 160 countries. It covers tuition, airfare, living expenses, and health insurance.

Learn more about the Fulbright Foreign Student Program →

GENERAL

Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program

Offers scholarships to Japanese mid-career professionals pursuing graduate degrees abroad in development related fields.

Learn more about the Joint Japan/World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program →

GENERAL

American Association of University Women (AAUW) International Fellowships

Provides funding for women pursuing graduate or postgraduate studies in the U.S.

Learn more about AAUW International Fellowships →

GENERAL

JASSO Scholarships

Japan Student Services Organization offers various scholarships for students studying abroad.

Learn more about JASSO Scholarships →

GENERAL

Toshizo Watanabe​ Study Abroad Scholarship Program

The scholarship grants awards to recipients in amounts determined by financial need and other qualifications.

Learn more about the Toshizo Watanabe Study Abroad Scholarship Program →

GENERAL

EducationUSA

This site lists scholarship search platforms for international students.

Learn more about EducationUSA informational resources →

Funding for Master's Students

Master's students can access scholarships, assistantships, and grants to support their education:

MASTER'S

Graduate Assistantships

Many U.S. universities offer teaching or research assistant positions, which cover tuition and provide a stipend.

Funding for PhD Students

PhD programs often come with robust funding packages:

PHD

University Fellowships

Many U.S. universities provide full tuition waivers and stipends for PhD students.

PHD

National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship

A highly competitive fellowship for STEM PhD students. Note: Only available for U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

Learn more about the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program →

Other Financial Resources

  • Outreach to Faculty: Speak with professors and advisors of your university to discuss any fellowships or funding.
  • Part-Time Work Opportunities: International students on an F-1 visa can work up to 20 hours per week on campus. Check with your university for job openings and requirements.
  • External Scholarships: Organizations like Scholarships.com and Fastweb list numerous scholarships available to international students.
