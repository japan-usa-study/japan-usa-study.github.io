Japan Related Programs and Centers

Explore Japanese studies programs at leading US universities

Japanese Centers at American universities offer a welcoming academic home base designed specifically to support students from Japan as they transition into U.S. campus life. These centers provide culturally responsive advising, language and academic resources, community-building events, and dedicated staff who understand the needs and expectations of Japanese learners. By connecting students with mentorship, peer networks, and programs that highlight both Japanese and American perspectives, these centers help make studying abroad more comfortable, enriching, and successful. For students seeking a supportive environment where they can thrive academically while maintaining a connection to their cultural identity, Japanese Centers serve as an ideal hub throughout their U.S. university experience.

Why Consider These Programs?

  • Access to world-class faculty and archival resources
  • Opportunities for fieldwork and study abroad in Japan
  • Exposure to a vibrant intellectual community
  • Specialized career support for Japan-related professions
